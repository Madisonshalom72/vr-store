🏗️ Create your store — two paths

Every store is born one of two ways. Both land on the same catalog, inventory, orders and agent machinery.

Arrange a store from platform templates. It receives a virtual address in the world plus a shareable store URL.

Path B · Scan My Store

Upload photos or a video walkthrough of your real shop — the platform rebuilds it as your virtual store. Demo: your photo becomes the store's world backdrop, with your catalog on the shelves.
